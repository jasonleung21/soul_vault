## Core principles (singles-specific)
- **When to come in**: only on a ball that lands inside your *attackable zone*, or after your deep shot has the opponent stretched. Otherwise hit deep and recover. (Collin Shick / Selkirk)
- **What to approach with**: deep and low, preferably DTL to the backhand. A hard approach comes back faster and cuts your closing time. A deep, heavy, slower ball buys you steps.
- **Follow the ball**: approach DTL means you shade to that side, bisecting the passing angles. Cover the DTL pass first: it's the shortest and fastest. The crosscourt pass travels further, so you have time.
- **Split at THEIR contact**, wherever you are. Stop when they hit, not when you arrive.
- **Singles net position**: about a step off the kitchen line (not toes on it) because you cover 20 ft and the lob alone. Move closer once you've read that the opponent can't lob.
- **First volley**: deep to the open corner, or a short angle/drop volley if they're deep. Never back to their middle. Re-bisect after every volley.
- **Ready position at net**: BH-based paddle position, with the two-hand block as the default for body balls.

## Drill library

| ID | Drill | Targets | Setup / rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Slide & Recover | L1 | Shadow → live. Ryan feeds wide FH; Son hits open stance, slides on the inner edge of the foot (no toe drag), explodes back to the centre cone before Ryan's next feed. 3×6 reps per side. Ryan watches the foot only |
| D2 | Attackable Zone Cone | L2 | Cone about 2 paces inside the baseline (adjust to your speed). Ryan feeds random depth. Ball lands inside the cone → approach. Outside → hit deep and recover. Ryan calls "wrong" on any bad decision. 3 sets of 10 |
| D3 | Approach + Split Stations | L2, L3 | Son approaches DTL. Ryan hits pass / dip / lob at random. Son must split as Ryan's paddle comes forward. Mark where the split lands with 3 floor markers (deep transition, mid, near kitchen) to learn your natural closing distance |
| D4 | Follow the Line / Bisect | L3, L6 | Son approaches DTL or crosscourt (alternate). Ryan tries to pass either side. Son must shade to the bisecting spot. Count passes beaten DTL vs CC |
| D5 | First Volley to Space | L5 | Son at net position. Ryan hits passing balls from the baseline. Son volleys to targets: deep open corner, or a short angle if Ryan stays deep. Middle-of-court volley = miss. 3×10 |
| D6 | Lob Alarm | L6 | Son at net. Ryan randomly lobs about 1 in 4. Son turns and crosses over (no backpedalling), then overheads or deep recovery. Find your singles net depth |
| D7 | Body Jam | L4 | Son at net. Ryan drives at the right hip / body. Rule: BH-based ready position, two-hand block or counter only. Start ¾ pace, then build |
| D8 | Return & Come In | L2, L3 | Live singles from the serve. When Son returns, he must hit deep and approach. When Son serves, he chooses a 3rd shot (deep drive + approach / drop + approach / stay back) by the ball's depth |
| D9 | Net King | All | Live points from a feed. Scoring: +2 point won at net, +1 point won from the back, −2 point lost after a bad approach decision (judged by Ryan). Game to 11 |

## 60-min session template
- 0–8 Warm-up: dynamic plus D1 shadow (footwork primer every session)
- 8–20 Block A: technique (closed drill)
- 20–40 Block B: semi-live (decision + random feed)
- 40–55 Block C: live game with scoring (transfers the skill to match conditions)
- 55–60 Debrief: 3 numbers (see Metrics) plus 1 cue to carry forward

## Metrics (rate-based, continues the 9/13 framework)
- **Approach win %** = points won after coming in ÷ approaches
- **Bad-approach rate** = approaches on balls outside the zone ÷ approaches
- **Loss type split**: passed DTL / passed CC / lobbed / dipped at feet / missed the approach
- **Split check**: was the split on time at the opponent's contact? (Ryan Y/N tally)
- Week 1 = baseline via one recorded singles match; re-measure in weeks 6 and 12
