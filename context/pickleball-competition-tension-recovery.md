# Competition Tension & Back/Hip Recovery

> Source: Soul 2026/11/01 conversation, after Richmond Hill Open (10/30–31, 34 games in 2 days).
> Not medical advice — working hypothesis. Physio if red flags below appear.

## Symptom (Jason's own words, 11/1)
- When nervous in competition, low back tightens and starts hurting **during** the game.
- Never happens in drilling or rec play.
- Gone after the match ("I guess I was relieved?").

## Why it likely happens
Key clue: same body, same strokes — only nerves differ, and it switches off when the threat is gone. Points to nervous-system-driven tension, not structure.

1. **Bracing when nervous (muscle guarding)** — stress raises baseline tone in trunk-protecting muscles (spinal erectors, quadratus lumborum, hip flexors). In rec they relax between points; in a tournament they stay partly on for a whole 11/15-pt game → fatigue → pain mid-game.
2. **Holding your breath** — breath-holding / high chest breathing under pressure makes trunk muscles do extra bracing; diaphragm stops helping.
3. **Lower, stiffer ready position** — nervous = lower and longer in stance, often bending through the lower back instead of hips. Fine for one rally, overloads low back + hip flexors over a game.
4. **Why it disappears after** — threat gone → nervous system turns tension down → muscles let go. "Relief" is physical, not just emotional.

## Recovery plan after Richmond Hill Open (Sun 11/1 – Wed 11/4)
Two separate problems: (a) in-game tension above, (b) plain overload (34 games in 2 days). This plan handles (b).

**Sun–Mon (11/1–11/2)**
- Walk 20–30 min; heat on low back 15 min, 1–2×/day.
- **90/90 breathing**: on back, feet on wall, hips & knees 90°. Slow nasal inhale, long 6–8 s exhale. 5 min, 2×/day (also trains the tension habit).
- Mobility 10–15 min: cat-cow, child's pose, half-kneeling hip flexor stretch (squeeze back-leg glute), figure-4 glute stretch, open-book rotations.
- Foam roll glutes + quads — NOT directly on lower back.
- Protein every meal, plenty of water, prioritize sleep.
- **Skip Monday Advanced 4.0+ at JEM.**

**Tue 11/3 — Pictopia, light only**
- Play only if resting pain ≤2/10. Dinks / drops, no hard firefights.
- Reactivate: dead bugs, glute bridges, bird-dogs, 2×10 each.

**Wed 11/4 — Ryan #9**
- Go if Tuesday didn't leave you worse next morning.
- Rest-of-week rule: pain during play ≤3/10 AND not worse next morning; fail either → another light day.

**Red flags → physio / urgent care**
- Not clearly better in 5–7 days → physio.
- Pain spreading down leg, numbness, tingling → physio.
- Wakes you at night → physio.
- Any loss of bladder/bowel control → urgent care immediately.

## Prevention for next tournament
- **Between points**: one physiological sigh (2 short nasal inhales + long mouth exhale); drop shoulders, soft belly, then set.
- **Ready position check**: hinge at hips & knees, not low back. Stand taller while partner serves; drop into stance only as the return leaves the paddle.
- **Waits between matches**: no 20+ min hunched on phone. Stay warm, walk; 2 min glute bridges + hip swings before being called.
- **Weekly off-court**: McGill big three (curl-up, side plank, bird-dog) — trunk endurance so bracing fatigues much later.
